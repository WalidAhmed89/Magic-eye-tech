# 🎥 Magic Eye Tech

A professional business website developed for a security camera installation and maintenance company. The website showcases the company's services, completed projects, and contact information, allowing potential customers to explore the business and easily get in touch.

One of the main features is an integrated contact form that automatically sends customer inquiries directly to the business owner via email, making communication simple and efficient.

**🌐 Live Demo:** https://magiceyetech.dpdns.org/

---

## ✨ Features

- 📹 Showcase completed camera installation projects.
- 🖼️ Display project images and videos.
- 📱 Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices.
- 📞 Quick contact through phone and WhatsApp.
- 🌐 Social media links for easy access.
- 📝 Contact form for customer inquiries.
- 📧 Automatic email delivery of contact form submissions to the business owner.

---

- EmailJS *(or your email service if different)*

## 📋 Contact Form

The website includes a fully functional contact form where visitors can submit:

- Full Name
- Phone Number / WhatsApp
- Email Address
- Requested Service
- Message

After submission, all information is automatically sent to the business owner's email, allowing for quick and organized communication.
